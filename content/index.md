This is a [digital garden](notes/digital-gardening.md): notes that grow over time instead of
being published once and abandoned. Some of it is finished. Most of it isn't. That's
the point.

Everything here carries a maturity marker — 🌱 seedling, 🌿 budding, 🌳 evergreen —
so you can tell a half-thought from a settled one.

## Where things live

| Section | What goes there |
| --- | --- |
| [Daily](daily/index.md) | The running log. Raw, dated, unedited. |
| [Notes](notes/index.md) | Evergreen ideas, one concept per note, heavily linked. |
| [Research](research/index.md) | Papers, experiments, open questions, literature notes. |
| [Reading](reading/index.md) | Books, articles, and what I actually took from them. |
| [Novel](novel/index.md) | Fiction in progress — outline, characters, scenes. |

## How to move through it

Use the search in the sidebar, or follow links. The **graph view** in the corner
shows how a note connects to the rest; the **backlinks** at the bottom of each page
show what points at it. Wandering is a legitimate way to read this.

Recently touched notes are the ones most worth reading — the garden is a
snapshot of what I'm thinking about now, not an archive.
