## The one-sentence version

_A [protagonist] wants [goal], but [obstacle], so they [action] — and the cost is [cost]._

## What the book is actually about

The plot is what happens. This is the question underneath it that the plot exists to
argue about. Write it as a question, not a theme — "themes" produce essays, questions
produce stories.
