One file per day, named `YYYY-MM-DD`. This is the inbox for everything: what I did,
what I read, what confused me, what I want to come back to.

These entries are deliberately low-stakes. Nothing here has to be good. The value is
that ideas get captured with a date attached, and the good ones get promoted later
into [evergreen notes](../notes/index.md) or [research](../research/index.md).

> [!tip] The promotion habit
> Once a week, skim the last seven daily notes. Anything you've now written down
> twice is trying to become a real note. Give it one.

## Capture prompts

If you're staring at a blank daily note, answer one of these:

- What's the most interesting thing I learned today, and why is it interesting?
- What did I get wrong, and what would I do differently?
- What question am I stuck on? Write the question precisely enough that a stranger could attack it.
- What did I read, and what's the one sentence worth keeping?
