# 2026-08-10

## What I'm thinking about

Set up this garden today. The interesting question isn't the tooling — it's whether
a dated log plus a linked note graph actually changes how I think, or just changes
where I type. [Networked thought](../notes/networked-thought.md) claims the former.
Worth testing over a few months rather than arguing about up front.

## Notes

- Quartz builds Markdown into a static site and keeps Obsidian's wikilinks intact,
  so the vault and the published site are the same files. No export step, which
  removes the usual excuse for letting a public site rot.
- The maturity markers (🌱 / 🌿 / 🌳) matter more than they look. They give permission
  to publish something unfinished, which is the whole bottleneck.
