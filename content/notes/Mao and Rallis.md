Mao and Rallis consider the comparison of $(G,G,\rho,0)$ and $(H = SL_2,1,0,\iota_{reg})$. In their setup, $(G,H)$ is a dual pair and $G \times H \into G'$ for some special $G'$ (To be concrete, $G$ is of $D_4,E_6,E_7,E_8$ type). 

To fit in Venkatesh's principle, $G \times H$ acts on $\Tilde{M} = \mathcal O_{min}(G')$. $\Tilde{M} //_{Wh}H = V_{\rho} = M$ and $\Tilde{M}//_{Wh} G:=\Tilde{M}//_{\mathcal O_G} G = T^*(SL_2 , \Psi) = M_1$ where $\mathcal O_G$ is a lifting of $\iota_{reg, SL_2}$, i.e. $\iota'$. In the end, $\Tilde{M}//_{Wh}(G \times H) = \mathcal J (SL_2)$. (All computed in [Hanzer–Savin, *Multiplicity free Weil representations arising from exceptional groups*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.01243), IMRN 2025)
