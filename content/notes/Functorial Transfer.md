
In BZSV it is suggested that if there is a $G^\vee \times H^\vee$ - action on a hyperspherical Hamiltonian space $M^\vee$, one should have a Hamiltonian theta correspondence between $G$ and $H$. 

And the theta correspondence is covered by $G^\vee \times H^\vee$ - action on $S$. ^Small-Project-

In general, one should get rid of the hypersphericality condition to explain more functorial transfers, for example the classical functorial transfer $G^\vee \times H^\vee$ -action on $T^*G$ where $H^\vee \into G^\vee$.
