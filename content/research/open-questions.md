# Open questions

A standing list. Questions get added freely and removed only when answered — with a
link to the note containing the answer. A question that sits here for a year is
either badly posed or genuinely hard; both are useful to notice.

## Currently open

- **Does note-taking method measurably affect output quality, or only felt productivity?**
  Suspect most of the evidence is self-reported and therefore worthless. What would a
  real study even look like?
- **What's the half-life of a note?** How long before a note is neither revisited nor
  linked? If it's short, capture volume is the wrong thing to optimise.
