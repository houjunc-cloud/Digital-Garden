[[rank one comparison + matching#^72ed67]]

An approach to prove $\Phi: Wh_{G_X} \isom Wh_{G}$ is:

>1. $Wh_{G_X}$ is isomorphic to $Wh_{G}$ over the regular locus 
>2. The isomorphism extends to the codimension one part
Then since both sides are normal affine, the isomorphism extends to the whole space.

Some remarks:

The term term can have different meanings (already for $M = T^*X$): $\mathfrak c_X = M//G$ or $X//N$, to take the moment map into account one should consider the former one, and for germ expansions the latter one instead.
