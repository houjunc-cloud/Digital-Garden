Deeper dives that don't fit in a daily note and aren't settled enough to be
[evergreen](../notes/index.md). Literature notes, experiment logs, and the questions
driving them.

## Structure

- **Literature notes** — one per paper. What it claims, how it argues it, whether I believe it.
- **Question notes** — one per open question, updated as evidence comes in.
- **Experiment logs** — hypothesis, setup, result, and what I'd change. Including the failures.

> [!important] Record negative results
> The experiments that didn't work are the ones you'll otherwise repeat in six months.
> A one-paragraph note saying "tried X, didn't work, here's why" pays for itself.

## Reading a paper into a note

Three passes: skim for the claim, read for the argument, then read the parts you
didn't believe. Only write the note after the third pass — writing during the first
just transcribes the abstract.

The note should answer: What's the claim? What would have to be true for it to hold?
What did they actually measure? What's the weakest link?
